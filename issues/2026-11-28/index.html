Calm Sea

Simon de Vlieger (Dutch, ca. 1600/1601–1653) · after 1640 · oil on wood · 37.5 × 44.5 cm · Metropolitan Museum 06.1200

Nearly all of it is sky. By the 1630s and 1640s de Vlieger was one of the best-known Dutch marine painters, and Willem van de Velde the Younger was his pupil. He and his sister Neeltje, a still-life painter, both took “de Vlieger,” the Flyer, as a professional name. An earlier picture of his, Christ calming the storm, borrowed its composition from a print by Adriaen Collaert. Keep that name for the next section.

The figures are tiny under an enormous sky, the scale you keep saving. Magellan named an ocean for weather like this, and it nearly killed his crew.

AnecdoteWednesday, 28 November 1520

Well named Pacific

Antonio Pigafetta of Vicenza went with Magellan as a volunteer and left the best account of the voyage. I’m quoting Lord Stanley of Alderley’s translation for the Hakluyt Society (1874).

When sailors doubted there was a way out, Magellan said he knew there was, “because he had seen it by a marine chart of the King of Portugal,” made by Martin of Bohemia. Stanley’s footnote is dry: Behaim’s globe of 1492 leaves out the whole of America. One ship, the San Antonio, put its captain in irons and went home. When a boat came back reporting the far cape and “the sea great and wide,” Magellan “began to cry,” and named it the Cape of Desire. Then:

Wednesday, the twenty-eighth of November, 1520, we came forth out of the said strait, and entered into the Pacific sea, where we remained three months and twenty days without taking in provisions or other refreshmentsPigafetta, tr. Stanley

They ate “old biscuit reduced to powder, and full of grubs,” and drank “water that was yellow and stinking.” They soaked the ox hides that wrapped the main-yard in the sea for four or five days, put them “a little on the embers,” and ate them. They ate sawdust. Rats sold for half a ducat each (Stanley renders it “half-a-crown”), “moreover enough of them were not to be got.” Gums swelled until men couldn’t eat, and nineteen died. In all that time they saw only two small uninhabited islands, which they called the Unfortunate Islands, and no storm at all:

This was well named Pacific … and I think that never man will undertake to perform such a voyage.

Adriaen Collaert after Jan van der Straet · Magellan Seated on Deck, from Americae Retectio · c. 1589 · engraving · Art Institute of Chicago 2023.316 · public domain

Look at the giant on the right. Pigafetta reports that when the Patagonians had a stomach-ache, they “put down their throats an arrow about two feet long.” He also wrote down their word for their great devil, Setebos. Richard Eden’s English version of 1577 is probably where Shakespeare found it, and Caliban invokes it in The Tempest.

Who has to be heard, and where

Los Molinos Mutual Water Co. v. Ekdahl

CourtNinth Circuit, No. 25-2302 · Nov. 24, 2026
HoldingReversed. California’s drought curtailment orders on Deer and Mill Creeks hit 22 water-rights holders individually, so they were adjudicatory, not legislative, and due process required a meaningful hearing beforehand. The claims aren’t moot, because the orders are “capable of repetition, yet evading review.”
Vote3–0. VanDyke wrote for Owens and Forrest. A separate memorandum affirmed dismissal of the takings claims.

To protect salmon and steelhead in 2021 and 2022, the State Water Resources Control Board ordered rights holders on the two creeks to cut or stop their diversions, with penalties of $1,000 a day and $2,500 per acre-foot. The plaintiffs say they asked to cross-examine the state’s witnesses and got five minutes of public comment. The line between rule and adjudication goes back to Bi-Metallic (1915), which said there must be “a limit to individual argument in such matters if government is to go on.” The panel’s answer: “We conclude that twenty-two water rights holders on Mill and Deer Creeks are ‘a few.’”

Five minutes without cross-examination is not a hearing “at a meaningful time and in a meaningful manner.”

Sunday Red, LLC v. Tigeraire, Inc.

CourtNinth Circuit, No. 25-2105 · Nov. 24, 2026
HoldingReversed. A California court has no personal jurisdiction over a Louisiana fan maker in a suit for a declaration of trademark non-infringement.
Vote2–1. Christen wrote for herself and Forrest; M. Smith dissented.

Two leaping tigers. Tigeraire, which makes fans that clip onto helmets and hats, chose its tiger for the LSU Tigers. Sunday Red, a TaylorMade golf-apparel company created with Tiger Woods, gave its tiger fifteen stripes, one per major. Two months after Sunday Red launched, Tigeraire sent a cease-and-desist letter, then opposed its trademark applications. Sunday Red sued at home. The majority says the inquiry looks at “the defendant’s contacts with the forum, not the defendant’s contacts with the plaintiff,” and counts no more than 80 products sold into California, one of them a T-shirt. The dissent counts 212 sales to Californians from January 2023 to October 2024, and calls Tigeraire’s conduct “a targeted scheme to attack a California company’s trademark rights”:

In one fell swoop, the majority casts doubt on decades of caselaw, redefines the legal landscape, and heightens the burdens IP holders must bear when seeking declaratory relief in their home states.

Poem · PolishMoscow, 1826

Cisza morska (Calm at Sea)

Adam Mickiewicz (1798–1855) · complete sonnet, from Sonety (Moscow, 1826), as transcribed on Polish Wikisource, old spelling kept · translation by Claude, unrhymed

The second of the eighteen Crimean Sonnets, written while Mickiewicz was living in Russia against his will. They were the first sonnet cycle in Polish. Yesterday’s Lermontov made a Russian version of one of them. The subtitle puts the ship off Tarkhankut. You saved a glowing hydra; here is another, in the last three lines.

na wysokości Tarkankut.

off Tarkhankut.

Już wstążkę pawilonu wiatr zaledwie muśnie,Cichemi gra pierśiami rozjaśniona woda;Jak marząca o szczęściu narzeczona młoda,Zbudzi się aby westchnąć, i wnet znowu uśnie.

Now the wind barely brushes the pennant’s ribbon;the brightened water plays with quiet breasts;like a young bride dreaming of happinessit wakes to sigh, and soon falls asleep again.

Żagle, nakształt chorągwi gdy wojnę skończono,Drzémią na masztach nagich; okręt lekkim ruchemKołysa się, jakgdyby przykuty łańcuchem;Majtek wytchnął, podróżne rozśmiało się grono.

The sails, like banners when the war is over,doze on the naked masts; with a light motionthe ship rocks, as if held fast by a chain;the sailor rests; the passengers break into laughter.

O morze! pośród twoich wesołych żyjątekJest polip co śpi na dnie gdy się niebo chmurzy,A na ciszę długiemi wywija ramiony.

O sea! among your merry little creaturesthere is a polyp that sleeps on the bottom when the sky clouds over,and in the calm unfurls its long arms.

O myśli! w twojéj głębi jest hydra pamiątek,Co śpi wpośród złych losów i namiętnéj burzy;A gdy serce spokojne, zatapia w niém szpony.

O thought! in your depths there is a hydra of memoriesthat sleeps through bad fortune and the storm of passion;and when the heart is calm, it sinks its claws in.

Haruomi Hosono, “Cosmic Surfin’”

Pacific · Haruomi Hosono, Shigeru Suzuki and Tatsuro Yamashita · CBS/Sony · released 21 June 1978 · instrumental

Three Tokyo musicians each composed their own tracks for an instrumental album about an ocean. The cover shows Motu Fara, a four-acre private island in Rangiroa’s Avatoru Pass, in French Polynesia: just the kind of island Magellan’s men never found. Hosono’s closing track turned up again under the same title, again credited to him, on Yellow Magic Orchestra’s debut, released 25 November 1978. You were pointed to Hosono House in October; this is the side door from city pop into YMO.

PaintingBorn today, 1757

St. Matthew

William Blake (British, 1757–1827) · 1799 · tempera on canvas · 38.5 × 26.5 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art 2017.4

Blake was born on 28 November 1757 at 28 Broad Street in Soho. Matthew isn’t writing here. The angel unrolls a scroll already lettered in red, and the evangelist presses his hands together as if the words were too much. Cleveland reads that awe as Blake’s own attitude toward inspiration, and notes that he reported visions of angels all his life. He was “not embraced by the fine art establishment,” but a few patrons commissioned pictures like this one.

Artifact70 cm of wood

Rei miro

Rapa Nui (Easter Island), Polynesia · 1800s · wood · 30.8 × 69.8 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art 1961.406

A crescent chest ornament; rei miro means “necklace of wood.” Cleveland says chiefly women of Rapa Nui generally wore them, and that the abstract forms at the tips replace the human heads more usual on these pieces. Magellan’s crossing met two uninhabited islands in the whole ocean. Rapa Nui lies in that same ocean.

Song“All in just three verses”

Martin Carthy & Dave Swarbrick, “Ship in Distress”

But Two Came By · Fontana · 1968 · trad. (Roud 807)

Carthy’s own summary: “Becalmed for days and out of food the crew of a ship draw lots for which of them shall die and serve as food for the rest.” The man chosen keeps a last watch and sees a rescue ship. A. L. Lloyd traced the story to a 16th-century Portuguese ballad, La Nau Catarineta, and a French one, La Courte Paille; Cecil Sharp thought it the grandest tune he found in Somerset. Your likes run to old narrative ballads. Carthy, recording it a third time in 2006: “for us it retains its majesty and its horror. And all in just three verses.”

Pastors want the disagreement shown, not settled

A Barna and Gloo survey of 442 Protestant pastors, reported on 26 November, found 94% worried that AI could misread Scripture. Asked about disputed passages, 60% want AI to lay out the main readings without declaring one correct, while only 5% want it to refuse the question. Barna’s summary: “let AI show you the disagreement, but don’t let it settle it for you.” (The pastors were surveyed in December 2025.)
