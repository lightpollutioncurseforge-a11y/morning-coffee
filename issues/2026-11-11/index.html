Landscape with Saint John the Baptist

Herri met de Bles (Flemish, 1510–1572) · c. 1540 · oil on wood · 30.5 × 42.3 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art 1967.20 · CC0

The owl, on the big rock at lower right. Cleveland says Bles was acclaimed in Italy as the Master of the Owl, il Civetta, after the bird he often put into his pictures.

Art historians call this a world landscape: a vast panorama with a religious story in the foreground, a type popularized by Joachim Patinir and fashionable all through the 16th century. The harbour city is imaginary. A small crowd against an enormous world, ships, orange against blue-green: your saved images are full of all three.

SongZamrock, 1975

Amanaz, “Khala My Friend”

Africa · Zambia Music Parlour · 1975 · reissued by Shadocks Music (2008) and Now-Again (2015)

From the Cambodian Rocks end of your likes: a reissue that rescued a scene. Amanaz formed in Kitwe, on the Copperbelt, in 1973, recorded its only album at the Malachite Film Studios in Chingola, and split in 1976. The name is an acronym: “Ask Me About Nice Artistes in Zambia.”

Zamrock came of age in the first decade after independence and rose and fell with the price of copper; Kaunda decreed that 95% of radio music be Zambian. The critic Mark Deming hears “deep, bluesy grooves with a strong psychedelic undercurrent and thick layers of fuzz guitar.” I haven’t heard it.

Plaque with Warrior and Attendants

Edo peoples, Court of Benin, Nigeria · ca. 1540–70 · brass · 47.6 × 38.1 cm · Ìgùn Ẹ́rọ̀nwwọ̀n (brass-casting guild) artists · Metropolitan Museum 1990.332 · public domain

Rank, cast in metal. Plaques like this were nailed to the pillars of the oba’s palace. The casters were a guild under royal decree, living in a quarter of the palace under the king’s direct control. Many show small groups arranged by rank around a central figure, identifiable by dress and emblem but not as individuals.

Your saved objects run to guardians and armed men. Most Benin plaques were taken by British forces in 1897. The Met’s page for this one wouldn’t load for me, so I can’t trace its route; its data says only “Gift of Mr. and Mrs. Klaus G. Perls, 1990.”

AnecdoteCape Cod, 11 November 1620

None had power to command them

Four hundred and six years ago today, by the calendar they used, the Mayflower lay at anchor in the wrong place. Storms had forced her into the hook of Cape Cod, and the patent the passengers carried was for Virginia. William Bradford, writing later, says why a compact was drawn up before anyone went ashore. It was

occasioned partly by the discontented and mutinous speeches that some of the strangers amongst them had let fall: that when they got ashore they would use their liberty that none had power to command them

The “strangers” were the non-Puritan passengers. The leading men also reckoned that a deed drawn up by themselves “would be as effective as any patent, and in some respects more so.” So, “in the name of God” and as “loyal subjects of our dread sovereign lord, King James,” 41 of the 101 passengers did “covenant and combine ourselves into a civil body politic,” promising “all due submission and obedience” to laws not yet written.

Within two or three months half the company was dead, and at the worst, Bradford writes, there were “but six or seven sound persons” to nurse the rest.

At the 300th anniversary Calvin Coolidge said the compact “has been designated as the first real constitution of modern times.” Bradford’s own account is plainer: order among people who had threatened to walk away from it. It sits near your Albion’s Seed and the Puritan reading after it.

Poem · Old IrishReichenau, 9th century

Pangur Bán

Anonymous Irish monk · six of eight quatrains · Old Irish text from Thurneysen’s Old Irish Reader, via Cambridge’s ASNC department · translation by Claude, unrhymed

The same century as the brooch below: a scholar’s room, like the ones you save, with a cat in it. It survives in a single manuscript, the notebook of an Irish monk at Reichenau Abbey, among notes on the Aeneid, hymns, Greek declensions and a tract on the nature of angels. Pangur, “White Pangur,” is the cat.

Messe [ocus] Pangur bán,cechtar nathar fria saindán;bíth a menma-sam fri seilgg,mu menma céin im saincheirdd.

I and white Pangur,each of us at his own art:his mind is set on hunting,mine on my own craft.

Caraim-se fos, ferr cach clú,oc mu lebrán léir ingnu;ní foirmtech frimm Pangur bán,caraid cesin a maccdán.

I love, better than any fame,to be still at my little book, hard at learning;white Pangur does not grudge it me:he loves his own boy’s game.

· · · one quatrain cut: the two of them alone in the house, never bored · · ·

Gnáth-húaraib ar gressaib galglenaid luch inna lín-sam;os mé, du·fuit im lín chéindliged n-doraid cu n-dronchéill.

Often, after his fits of battle,a mouse sticks in his net;and into my net, for my part,falls a hard rule with a stubborn sense.

Fúachid-sem fri frega fála rosc anglése comlán;fúachimm chéin fri fégi fismu rosc réil, cesu imdis.

He trains against the wallhis full, bright eye;I train against the edge of knowingmy own clear eye, weak as it is.

· · · one quatrain cut: the joy of the catch, and of a problem solved · · ·

Cía beimmi amin nach ré,ní·derban cách ar chéle.Maith la cechtar nár a dán,subaigthius a óenurán.

Though we are like this all the time,neither one gets in the other’s way.Each of us likes his artand takes his joy in it alone.

Hé fesin as choimsid dáuin muid du·n-gní cach óenláu;du thabairt doraid du gléfor mu mud céin am messe.

He himself is masterof the work he does each day;to bring what is hard into the clearis my own work.

The benchRead from the opinion

Latitude, not carte blanche

In re Application of the United States (LinkedIn)

CourtFourth Circuit, No. 26-4303 · Nov. 10, 2026 · published
HoldingA provider may challenge a Stored Communications Act order as overbroad. This disclosure order and its one-year gag order must be narrowed on remand. Denial of the government’s ex parte filings to LinkedIn affirmed.
Vote3–0. Wilkinson wrote, joined by Wynn and Berner.
BelowE.D. Va. (Newport News), District Judge Elizabeth W. Hanes, adopting a magistrate judge’s recommendation.

A news outlet published classified details of what it called a top-secret military operation abroad. Investigating the leak, the government got an order making LinkedIn hand over 18 months of metadata for the outlet’s journalists: everyone they connected or messaged with. In that time they published nearly 1,000 articles, less than 5% apparently related. A second order barred LinkedIn from telling anyone for a year.

The district court said LinkedIn could complain only of logistical burden. The panel disagreed: irrelevance is itself an “undue burden,” and the First Amendment, through Justice Powell’s Branzburg concurrence, sharpens the tailoring. Wilkinson concedes a great deal first:

The government is entitled to discovery to the full extent permitted by law. It is entitled to a disclosure order. It is even entitled to a broad disclosure order. But it is not entitled to carte blanche.

On the gag order, a prior restraint by both sides’ agreement, LinkedIn asked only to tell one previously vetted lawyer for the outlet. Unless the government does better on remand, that exception “must be adopted.” He stresses national security again (“To repeat to the point of testing reader patience,” he writes) and ends: “There is space under law for both.”

Artifact9.5 cm across

Open-ring brooch

Pictish or Irish · early 800s · silver, cast and partially gilded; amber · 9.3 × 9.5 cm · Ireland (Galway) per the Met’s record · Metropolitan Museum 1981.413 · public domain

A cloak pin with a liability rule. Men wore these singly at the shoulder and women on the breast, pin pointing up, and an Irish law code says that if the pin injures someone, the wearer isn’t at fault provided it didn’t project too far and was worn the proper way.

Brooches in precious metal were marks of status. The Senchas Mór says the sons of major kings in fosterage should have “brooches of gold having crystal inserted in them,” sons of minor kings only silver. Amber was one of the materials set in these studs. Most of your saved objects are things you could hold; this one you’d wear.

The Fureys and Davey Arthur, “The Green Fields of France”

The Green Fields of France · Banshee · 1979 · written by Eric Bogle in 1976 as “No Man’s Land”

Near “Down in a Willow Garden” in your likes: a ballad at a young soldier’s grave. Bogle wrote it after he and his wife visited three or four military cemeteries in France and Flanders. He picked “Willie McBride” because it rhymed with “grave side,” and made him Irish to counter the anti-Irish feeling in Britain then. The Fureys’ version was the big success.

Painting62 × 85 cm

The North Cape by Moonlight

Peder Balke (Norwegian, 1804–1887) · 1848 · oil on canvas · 62 × 85 cm · Metropolitan Museum 2019.167.4 · public domain

The far north, a small boat, a torn sky: your saved images keep coming back to all three. Farmers in Toten paid for Balke’s training, and he decorated their farms in return. In 1846 he sold thirty paintings to Louis Philippe for Versailles.

Later he bought farmland outside Christiania and laid out Balkeby, a suburb where workers could borrow from him to build their own houses, with wide streets against fire. In June 1879 many of the houses burned anyway.

“These tools will never sit on the bench”

Marvic Leonen, senior associate justice of the Philippine Supreme Court, to the Integrated Bar’s disciplinary investigators: “These tools will never sit on the bench. They should never sign your reports.” He told them to verify every citation against its source and to disclose AI use. His sharper line was about people: “Deference feels like courtesy. Going along feels like collegiality. The status quo never demands that we do wrong. It asks only that we do nothing.”

Puerto Rico: no exemption for representing yourself

Puerto Rico’s Supreme Court fined two siblings $500 each in an inheritance dispute. Representing themselves, they filed papers that cited nearly 25 cases that didn’t exist or were misdescribed. Justice Raúl Candelario López, concurring, said the citations appeared machine-generated. The court drew its line between drafting with AI and filing its output unchecked. I read the San Juan Daily Star’s report of the Oct. 28 decision, not the decision.
