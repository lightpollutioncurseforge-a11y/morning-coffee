Arion on a Sea Horse

William-Adolphe Bouguereau (French, 1825–1905) · 1855 · oil on fabric · one of a pair, 71.3 × 111.8 cm unframed · Cleveland Museum of Art 1980.238 · CC0

Arion, the singer, escaping pirates. Herodotus tells it: sailing home to Corinth with a fortune, Arion learned the crew meant to take his money and throw him overboard. They told him to kill himself or jump. He asked to sing first, in all his regalia, from the half-deck, and they drew back to hear “the best singer in the world.” He sang, and leapt; “a dolphin (so the story goes) took Arion on his back.” Godley’s note calls the song “a high-pitched (and apparently very well-known) song or hymn in honor of Apollo,” which puts it near the Delphic Hymn in your likes.

Bouguereau swapped the dolphin for a sea-horse. Cleveland says this pair (a bacchante on a panther is the other) came from a set of eight made for a Paris house decorated in the Pompeian style, with gold leaf “calculated to come to life” under the gaslight.

AnecdoteOcracoke Inlet, 22 November 1718

“Damnation seize my Soul”

On this date, by the Old Style calendar, Lieutenant Robert Maynard of HMS Pearl caught Edward Teach, called Blackbeard, at Ocracoke Inlet in North Carolina. Virginia’s governor had hired two sloops, crewed from his warships, with no guns mounted. The account below is from the General History of the Pyrates, signed “Captain Charles Johnson” and printed in London in 1724.

Johnson says Teach had about 25 men, “tho’ he gave out” it was 40, and spent the night before drinking. Asked whether his wife knew where his money was buried, he said: “no Body but himself and the Devil, knew where it was, and the longest Liver should take all.” In the morning Teach’s sloop ran aground, and Maynard, drawing more water, threw his ballast and water overboard so he could come at him. Teach hailed him, then:

Black-beard took a Glass of Liquor, and drank to him with these Words: Damnation seize my Soul if I give you Quarters, or take any from you.Johnson, 1724. Maynard answered that “he expected no Quarters from him, nor should he give him any.”

A broadside killed or wounded twenty of Maynard’s men. He sent the rest below, and when Teach boarded through the smoke of his own grenades (“Case Bottles fill’d with Powder”), they came up the ladders. Teach fell “as he was cocking another Pistol,” with five-and-twenty wounds by Johnson’s count. His head went on the bowsprit. Johnson adds that some of Maynard’s men “went afterwards a pyrating themselves.”

Wikipedia notes that the sources disagree: Maynard reported 8 of his men and 12 pirates killed; Spotswood, ten and ten. It also finds “no supporting evidence” for at least one of Johnson’s stories, that Teach married a planter’s daughter.

SongBahia, 1954

Dorival Caymmi, “É Doce Morrer no Mar”

On Canções Praieiras (Beach Songs), Odeon, 1954 · his first album · all songs by Caymmi

The title means “It’s sweet to die at sea,” which is what Arion bet on. The other seven include “O Mar,” “Pescaria (Canoeiro)” and “A Jangada Voltou Só” (“The raft came back alone”). Rolling Stone Brasil ranked the album 77th of all Brazilian records. Back in 1935, Portuguese Wikipedia says, he hosted a Bahia radio show called Caymmi e Suas Canções Praieiras. A discovery pick, from the side of your likes that goes looking for old regional records. I haven’t heard it.

Who is present, and who may sell

Texas v. Blanche

CourtFifth Circuit, en banc, No. 24-10386 · Nov. 20, 2026
HoldingHouse members voting by proxy can’t be counted toward a quorum. The Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2023 was passed unconstitutionally. The enrolled-bill rule doesn’t bar the challenge. The injunction against enforcing the Pregnant Workers Fairness Act against Texas, and only Texas, is affirmed.
Vote9–8. Wilson for Elrod, Jones, Smith, Willett, Ho, Duncan, Engelhardt and Oldham. Graves dissents, joined by Stewart, Richman, Southwick, Higginson, Douglas and Ramirez. Haynes dissents in a footnote.

The House passed the Act 225–201 under its COVID rule. Of the 427 members recorded, 226 voted by proxy, so fewer than half were physically there. Wilson:

“Proxy presence” does not count. While the chambers of the legislature have much discretion regarding their rules of procedure, the Quorum Clause’s constraints on congressional power cannot be circumvented by congressional fiat.

On emergencies he quotes a 1917 case: an emergency “may not call into life a power which has never lived.” In a footnote he adds that on the government’s view “the House is free to let 434 Representatives give the Speaker their proxies.” Graves says the majority has written a physical-presence requirement into the Clause, and asks why a law invalid in passage is enjoined in one sliver for one State. His verdict: “illogical, inexplicable, and invalid.” Haynes’s footnote says she “does not think it is appropriate to throw out the Federal House of Representatives in this arena.”

United States v. Deare

CourtFifth Circuit, No. 24-30241 · Nov. 19, 2026 · per curiam
Holding“The plain text of the Second Amendment covers the sale of firearms.” Denial of the motion to dismiss an unlicensed-dealing charge vacated and remanded. Whether the licensing law fits historical tradition is left for remand.
Vote2–1. Ho concurring; Oldham, joined by Ho, concurring in the judgment; Haynes dissenting. The opinion doesn’t say whether Oldham joined the per curiam.

Haynes would affirm, since the Second Amendment “doesn’t mention sellers.” Ho: “By that logic, the First Amendment doesn’t mention sellers, so presumably booksellers aren’t covered by the First Amendment, either.” Oldham reaches past guns to speech and church:

These examples demonstrate clear principles. A right to property, free speech, corporate worship, and firearms includes the right to provide these things. And to acquire them.

He calls the circuit’s 2025 Peterson decision “gravely mistaken” and asks that it be overruled.

Poem · SpanishAnonymous romance

El conde Arnaldos · Count Arnaldos

Complete as printed on Spanish Wikisource, which names no edition · translation by Claude, unrhymed

The other singer at sea. A sailor’s song calms the water and brings up the fish, and he won’t hand it over. Wikisource files it under anonymous romances and medieval literature.

¡Quién hubiese tal venturasobre las aguas del mar,como hubo el conde Arnaldosla mañana de San Juan!Andando a buscar la cazapara su falcón cebar,vio venir una galeraque a tierra quiere llegar;las velas trae de seda,la ejarcia de oro tozal.

Who could have such fortuneupon the waters of the seaas Count Arnaldos hadon the morning of Saint John!Out hunting for gameto feed his falcon,he saw a galley comingthat wants to come to land;its sails are silk,its rigging gold.

Marinero que la guíadiciendo viene un cantarque la mar ponía en calma,los vientos hace amainar;los peces que andan al hondo,arriba los hace andar,las aves que van volando,al mástil vienen posar.

The sailor steering hercomes singing a songthat laid the sea calm,makes the winds drop;the fish that swim in the deepit makes rise to the top,the birds that go flyingcome and settle on the mast.

Allí habló el conde Arnaldos,bien oiréis lo que dirá:—“Por tu vida el marinerodígasme ora ese cantar”.Respondióle el marinero,tal respuesta le fue a dar:—“Yo no digo mi canciónsino a quien conmigo va”.

Then Count Arnaldos spoke;you will hear well what he said:“By your life, sailor,sing me now that song.”The sailor answered him,and this was the answer he gave:“I do not sing my songexcept to one who comes with me.”

“Ejarcia” is rigging. I couldn’t find a source glossing “tozal” here, so I’ve left it out of the English.

Court lady singing and playing the vina

Attributed to Muhammad Rizavi Hindi · India, Mughal · c. 1760 · gum tempera, ink and gold on paper · page 31.1 × 26.2 cm · Cleveland Museum of Art 2013.349 · CC0

Today is the feast of Cecilia, patron of musicians, so here is a singer. Cleveland’s tell: “When the mouth is open, and teeth and tongue are showing, she is singing.” She strums a three-string vina with a gourd resonator. On the back are Persian lines, one from Jami, in Cleveland’s translation: “Until you are bold enough to take to the seas, / You will not reap spiritual benefits from your inner heart.” You saved a Persianate page of Bahram Gur; this is its Indian cousin, with the sea turning up again.

SongLondon, 1692

Henry Purcell, “Wondrous machine!”

From Hail! Bright Cecilia, Z. 328 · text by Nicholas Brady · David Thomas, bass; English Baroque Soloists; John Eliot Gardiner (Erato) · MusicBrainz lists it on a 2007 Purcell set and the 2023 Gardiner Erato box. I couldn’t confirm the original recording year.

Purcell wrote the ode for the London Cecilian feast; it premiered at Stationers’ Hall on 22 September 1692 and was encored. You liked Dido’s Lament. This bass air is the organ’s song, and it fits your question about machines and authority: Brady calls the organ a “Wondrous Machine!” whose notes “From Heav’n... were giv’n,” and Wikipedia notes Brady followed Dryden in crediting Cecilia with inventing the organ. Gardiner’s recording is one of several; I haven’t heard any of them.

Nautilus Cup

Cornelis Jansz van der Burch (Dutch, active in Delft, d. 1615) · 1607 · silver-gilt, nautilus shell · Cleveland Museum of Art 2023.154 · CC0

A drinking cup with a sea monster built in. Cleveland says nautilus shells reached the Netherlands from Indonesia, and Delft silversmiths mounted them for rich collectors; the early Delft ones “feature the dramatic motif of a sea monster’s head.” Only a few others from Delft in that period are known. A glass for a toast like Teach’s, with a sea monster grinning at the rim.

AIAuthority and law

Geneva agrees to “elaborate,” not “negotiate”

A follow-up on the weapons talks. Early on Saturday, after running past their deadline, the 128 states party to the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons “agreed to launch the elaboration of an international instrument on lethal autonomous weapons systems,” to run over two years, the conference president told reporters, per AFP. Some countries objected to the word “negotiate,” so delegates settled on “elaborate.” Stop Killer Robots called it “a watered-down mandate that fails to chart a clear path towards a legally binding instrument,” and says over 95 states backed a treaty during the week. I couldn’t find the official decision text.

Judges in a chatbot pilot

The Philadelphia Inquirer reports that around 25 judges in Pennsylvania and nearby states, among them federal judges in the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, are in Penn Law’s AI Testbed, using chatbots to schedule hearings, draft jury instructions and recall case facts. Judge Joshua D. Wolson: “I may have the AI help me wordsmith while I have my law clerks work on the legal analysis piece of it. Frankly, it’s faster.” He doesn’t use it for legal research or drafting opinions. Penn’s Polk Wagner, who runs it: “These tools are pretty bad at legal research — even today.” A bar committee co-chair knows of no court-wide rules.
